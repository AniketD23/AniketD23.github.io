I am a Ph.D. student in computer architecture in the [Siebel School of Computing and Data Science](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/) at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) advised by [Professor Saugata Ghose](https://ghose.cs.illinois.edu/). My research interests are in intelligent and sustainable memory and storage systems. I received my B.S. (also at Illinois!) in Computer Science and Philosophy.
